## Introduction

今天换到生态主题：不同物种真的能互相替代吗？这篇研究报道适合练习区分总体趋势、特殊情况与证据局限，尤其要留意“结果显著”与“证据充分”并不是一回事。

训练重点：数字定位、同义替换、True / False / Not Given 和研究限制。

## Reading comprehension

Answer questions 1, 3, and 4 briefly in English. For questions 2 and 5, choose **TRUE**, **FALSE**, or **NOT GIVEN** according to the original article.

**1. How many published studies were included in the analysis?**

{{< answer >}}
423 published studies.

定位：研究规模段落，注意区分研究篇数与数据点数量。

**2. Most ecological benefits stopped increasing once a few species were present.**

{{< answer >}}
**FALSE.** Most benefits continued increasing with diversity.

定位：总体趋势段落，判断增加趋势是否在少数物种出现后停止。
{{< /answer >}}

**3. What limits confidence in the finding about ocean carbon storage?**

{{< answer >}}
It relies on relatively few datasets.

定位：海洋结果之后的限制说明。
{{< /answer >}}

**4. Why can protection against coastal flooding depend less on total biodiversity?**

{{< answer >}}
A few essential species may provide that service.

定位：例外情况段落，关注基础性物种的作用。
{{< /answer >}}

**5. The researchers calculated the financial cost of restoring every damaged ecosystem.**

{{< answer >}}
**NOT GIVEN.** No such cost estimate is provided.

缺少信息不等于作者否定了这一说法。
{{< /answer >}}

## Reading strategy

先定位研究规模，再区分总体趋势、特殊情况与证据限制。第 3 题要把结果的强弱与证据的多少分开；第 5 题不要把原文未提供的信息推断为错误。

## Review
