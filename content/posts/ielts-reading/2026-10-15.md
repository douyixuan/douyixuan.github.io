## Reading comprehension

For questions 1–3, choose **TRUE**, **FALSE**, or **NOT GIVEN**. Answer questions 4–5 briefly in English.

**1. Traditional models treated growth rates as broadly uniform across a forest.**

{{< answer >}}
**TRUE.** They assumed similar growth throughout.
{{< /answer >}}

**2. The model overestimated biomass in the edge plots by 12%.**

{{< answer >}}
**FALSE.** It underestimated biomass by 12%.
{{< /answer >}}

**3. The researchers measured exactly 100 trees.**

{{< answer >}}
**NOT GIVEN.** The article supplies no tree count.
{{< /answer >}}

**4. How did Yin check the model's accuracy?**

{{< answer >}}
She compared its estimates with her field measurements.
{{< /answer >}}

**5. Name two environmental conditions that might make edge trees grow differently.**

{{< answer >}}
Any two: more sunlight, wind exposure, or fertilizer runoff.
{{< /answer >}}

## Reading strategy

注意第 2 题的方向词：百分比一致，不代表判断正确。第 5 题问的是可能影响因素，回答时不要擅自写成已证明的因果关系。

## Expression practice

**underestimate X by Y%** — 将 X 低估了 Y%。

Original practice sentence: Our first plan underestimated the time needed by 20%.

试着将 underestimate 换成 overestimate，并解释意思如何变化。
