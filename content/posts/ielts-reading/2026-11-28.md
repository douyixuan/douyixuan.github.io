## Reading plan

**Suggested level:** B1–B2. **Time:** 4 minutes to read, 5 minutes for questions.

## Introduction

一条河被水坝拦住近百年。第一座坝拆掉后，巡河的人看见了鲑鱼。读的时候想一想：这次目击证明了什么？又有什么，还得继续观察才能知道？

## Vocabulary

- **upstream**：向上游。
- **survey**：调查；这里指定期巡查河段。
- **sediment**：泥沙、沉积物。
- **migration**：洄游；也可指迁徙。
- **authorize**：正式批准。
- **barrier**：障碍；文中指挡住鱼群的坝。

## English retelling / 英文故事式复述（非原文）

根据美国国家公园管理局奥林匹克国家公园的[原始报道（2012 年 8 月 20 日）](https://www.nps.gov/olym/learn/news/return-of-the-kings.htm)及[河流修复资料](https://www.nps.gov/olym/learn/nature/elwha-ecosystem-restoration.htm)独立复述。不转载原文或图片，不代表 NPS 背书。

<section class="ielts-source-text" aria-label="English retelling, not the original article">
<p>In August 2012, Phil Kennedy was looking for salmon on Washington State’s Elwha River. His fisheries team had been checking the river every week. Then he caught sight of adult Chinook salmon inside Olympic National Park, about two miles upstream from the park boundary. The lower Elwha Dam had been gone for less than five months.</p>
<p>A century earlier, two dams had supplied electricity for the growing area around Port Angeles. They also cut off much of the salmon’s route to the upper river. Sediment collected behind the dams instead of travelling downstream, and the reservoirs covered places important to the Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe. Congress authorized restoration in 1992, but work on removing the dams did not start until 2011.</p>
<p>The lower dam came out first. Fish could reach part of the river that had been closed to them, even while the upper dam at Glines Canyon remained. That second barrier was removed in 2014. The two removals allowed the river to flow all the way from the Olympic Mountains to the sea again.</p>
<p>Kennedy’s sighting answered one question: adult Chinook could find their way back into the park. It did not show how many salmon would return or how well the river as a whole would recover. To answer those questions, the team needed observations over time. The sighting was a reason to keep watching, not a reason to stop.</p>
</section>
