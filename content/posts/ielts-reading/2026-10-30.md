## Introduction

## Vocabulary

- **permeability** — 渗透性；流体通过材料的容易程度。
- **selectivity** — 选择性；筛选特定对象的能力。
- **membrane** — 膜；薄层材料。
- **groove** — 沟槽；凹槽。
- **vortex** — 涡旋。
- **reservoir** — 储液池；蓄水池。

## Reading comprehension

Answer questions 1–3 briefly in English. For questions 4–5, choose **TRUE**, **FALSE**, or **NOT GIVEN**. These are original practice questions, not official IELTS questions.

**1. Why can larger openings be a disadvantage in a filter?**

{{< answer >}}
They allow more unwanted particles through. Locate the comparison in the first subsection.
{{< /answer >}}

**2. Why did the team add dye to the experimental water?**

{{< answer >}}
To make the flow visible. Distinguish a measurement aid from a filtering component.
{{< /answer >}}

**3. What formed near the grooves when pumping became faster?**

{{< answer >}}
Vortices. Check the paragraph contrasting faster and slower pumping.
{{< /answer >}}

**4. The swirling flow stopped both particles and water from passing through.**

{{< answer >}}
**FALSE.** Water could pass while particles were redirected. “Both” makes the statement incorrect.
{{< /answer >}}

**5. The article reports the price of a commercial filter made using these guidelines.**

{{< answer >}}
**NOT GIVEN.** No commercial price is supplied. Absence of a price does not mean a product is inexpensive.

## Reading strategy

做判断题时，先检查题干中的范围词，例如 **both、all、only**。看到原文出现相关名词，还不能判定 TRUE：必须比较完整命题。找不到某项信息时，区分“明确否定”和“根本未提及”。

## Expression practice

以下例句均为自拟，并非原文引文。

**a trade-off between A and B** — A 与 B 之间的取舍。

Example: Choosing a smaller suitcase involves a trade-off between capacity and convenience.

**rather than** — 而不是。

Example: Compare the evidence rather than relying on the headline.
