## Reading comprehension

These are original practice questions, not official IELTS questions. For questions 1, 4, and 5, choose **TRUE**, **FALSE**, or **NOT GIVEN**.

**1. The new system keeps its water-production rate constant.**

{{< answer >}}
**FALSE.** Its production adjusts as available solar power changes.

定位：开头的新系统介绍。
{{< /answer >}}

**2. Short answer — NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS: What force removes salt ions during electrodialysis?**

{{< answer >}}
**electric field**

定位：Pump and flow。勿与反渗透使用的压力混淆。
{{< /answer >}}

**3. Sentence completion — ONE NUMBER: The earlier design recalculated its operating settings every ___ minutes.**

{{< answer >}}
**3**

定位：Pump and flow 的旧方案说明。注意题目问旧方案。
{{< /answer >}}

**4. During the trial, the new prototype directly used more than 94% of the panels' electrical output on average.**

{{< answer >}}
**TRUE.**

定位：Solar commands。这是电能利用比例，不是脱盐率。
{{< /answer >}}

**5. The article gives an exact retail price for a commercial unit.**

{{< answer >}}
**NOT GIVEN.** No exact retail price is supplied.

定位：文末的后续开发计划。
{{< /answer >}}

## Reading strategy

给每个数字标注“对象、单位、新方案或旧方案”。不要把运行指标当作水质指标，也不要把未来开发计划当作已经上市。

## Expression practice

以下例句为自拟，并非原文引文。

**in response to** — 响应；作为对……的反应。

Example: I changed my study plan in response to feedback.

**rather than** — 而不是。

Example: Check the relevant paragraph rather than relying on memory.

## Review

标出每题定位段落，解释 **FALSE** 是证据相反，**NOT GIVEN** 是证据不足。
