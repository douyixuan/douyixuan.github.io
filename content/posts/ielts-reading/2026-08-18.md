## Today's article

**“These 3D-printed objects can tell you if they’re being used properly”** — Adam Zewe, *MIT News*, August 5, 2026.

**Original article:** https://news.mit.edu/2026/shiftlens-3d-printed-objects-can-tell-you-if-used-properly-0805

## Introduction

MIT researchers developed **ShiftLens**, a design and fabrication system for producing 3D-printed objects that can change their visible surface appearance when users press, slide, turn, or tighten them. The key idea is that the interaction is completely mechanical: no sensors, screens, or electronics are required.

The article is useful IELTS practice because it combines concrete examples with explanations of design constraints, tradeoffs, and possible applications.

## Vocabulary

1. **fabricate** — make or manufacture something, especially using an industrial process.
2. **interactive** — able to respond to a user's actions.
3. **mechanically switchable** — able to change state through physical movement rather than electronics.
4. **fragile** — easily damaged or broken.
5. **optical layer** — a material layer that controls or alters how light is seen.
6. **lenticular lens** — a small curved lens that shows different images depending on viewing angle.
7. **backplane** — an underlying surface or layer that supports another component.
8. **align** — arrange parts so that they are in the correct relative position.
9. **linkage** — a mechanical connection between moving parts.
10. **actuation mechanism** — a component that produces or controls physical movement.
11. **rapid prototyping** — quickly producing early versions of a design for testing.
12. **streamline** — make a process simpler, faster, or more efficient.

## Reading comprehension

**1. What problem is ShiftLens designed to solve?**

{{< answer >}}
It makes it easier to create interactive physical objects that can visibly indicate their state without relying on screens, sensors, or electronic circuits.
{{< /answer >}}

**2. How does ShiftLens change an object's appearance?**

{{< answer >}}
It combines a layer of tiny lenticular lenses with a patterned layer underneath. When the two layers move relative to one another, different parts of the underlying image become visible, causing the surface appearance to change.
{{< /answer >}}

**3. Why can a fully mechanical display be useful in harsh environments?**

{{< answer >}}
Electronic components can be damaged by water, chemicals, pressure, twisting, or impact. A mechanical system can be more durable because it does not depend on fragile circuits or screens.
{{< /answer >}}

**4. What is one major limitation of the current ShiftLens system?**

{{< answer >}}
It requires some kind of physical shifting motion between the optical layers, so it cannot be applied directly to every object unless the object already moves in a suitable way or an additional mechanism is added.
{{< /answer >}}

**5. Give one example of a practical application described in the article.**

{{< answer >}}
One example is a chemical bottle that changes color and displays a check mark when its cap is securely tightened, but shows a warning symbol when the cap is loose.
{{< /answer >}}
