## Reading comprehension

Answer questions 1–3 briefly in English. For questions 4–5, choose **TRUE**, **FALSE**, or **NOT GIVEN** according to the original article's main text.

**1. What did earlier explanations suggest the white lime fragments revealed?**

{{< answer >}}
Careless mixing or inferior ingredients.

**2. How can water help repair a crack?**

{{< answer >}}
It reacts with the fragments, producing a calcium-rich liquid. Calcium carbonate can then crystallize and seal the gap.

**3. How long did the experimental cracks take to close?**

{{< answer >}}
No more than two weeks.
{{< /answer >}}

**4. The comparison sample without quicklime also stopped water from passing through.**

{{< answer >}}
**FALSE.** Its cracks remained unhealed, and water continued to pass through.
