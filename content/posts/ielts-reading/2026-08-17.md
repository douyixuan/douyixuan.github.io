## Today's article

**“I hate what AI is doing to the minds and happiness of the young”** — Katherine Rundell, *The Guardian*, August 8, 2026.

This is an opinion essay suitable for roughly IELTS 7.5+. Rundell argues that the value of education does not lie only in producing a correct essay or answer. Reading, writing, struggling with ideas, and learning to think independently are themselves part of the educational outcome.

A useful way to read the piece is to identify its structure: **claim → evidence → analogy → conclusion**. Also ask whether the argument establishes that AI itself harms learning, or instead that poorly designed use of AI can harm learning.

## Vocabulary

1. **authoritarian** — favoring strict obedience to a powerful central authority.
2. **destabilise** — make a system or situation less stable.
3. **unprecedented** — never having happened or existed before.
4. **surveillance** — close observation or monitoring of people or activity.
5. **robust** — strong and reliable; able to withstand difficulty.
6. **infiltrate** — gradually enter or spread through a place or system.
7. **irreparable** — impossible to repair or correct.
8. **cognitive offloading** — transferring mental work to an external tool or system.
9. **mastery** — comprehensive knowledge or skill in a subject.
10. **resilience** — the ability to recover from or continue through difficulty.
11. **scrutiny** — careful and critical examination.
12. **emancipation** — freedom from restriction, control, or dependence.

## Reading comprehension

1. Why does Rundell distinguish between producing a good essay and actually learning through writing?
2. What does she mean by **“cognitive surrender”**, and how is it different from ordinary cognitive offloading?
3. Why does the author think poorer schools may be more vulnerable to excessive AI use?
4. What is the purpose of her analogy comparing AI use with taking a bus instead of training for a race?
5. Does her argument prove that AI itself harms learning, or mainly that **poorly designed AI use** harms learning? Explain using evidence from the article.

## Expression of the day

> **The process is the point.**

A useful IELTS Writing adaptation:

> In education, efficiency should not always be the primary goal, because the learning process itself develops resilience, judgment and independent thought.
