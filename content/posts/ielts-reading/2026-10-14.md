## Reading strategy

**FALSE** 需要原文反驳；信息缺失才是 **NOT GIVEN**。第 5 题保留 **may**，别把可能解释写成已证实的因果关系。

## Expression of the day

**may be less susceptible to…** — 可能较不容易受到……的影响。

Original practice sentence: A well-tested system may be less susceptible to unexpected failures.

Use **may** when presenting a plausible explanation rather than an established cause.
